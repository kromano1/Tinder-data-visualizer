How to get your data

  1. 1
    Go to account.gotinder.com/data and sign in.
  2. 2
    Request your data. Tinder emails you a download link, usually within a few days.
  3. 3
    Download the .zip and drop it above. The data.json inside it works too.

Your privacy

  • Your file is analyzed entirely inside your browser. It is never uploaded to this website or anyone else.
  • The page is locked down so it cannot make network requests at all, so it has no way to send your data anywhere.
  • No accounts, cookies, analytics, ads or trackers. Close or refresh the tab and your data is gone.
  • Exports are created on your device and saved straight to your downloads.
