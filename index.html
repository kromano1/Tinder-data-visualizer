Swipe Stats

See what your swipes say about you

Drop in your Tinder data export to see your swipes, matches, conversations and habits as charts. Everything runs in your browser. Your data is never uploaded anywhere.

How to get your data

  1. Go to account.gotinder.com/data and sign in.
  2. Request your data. Tinder emails you a download link, usually within a few days.
  3. Download the .zip file and drop it above. You can also drop just the data.json file inside it.

Swipe Stats is an independent project and is not affiliated with Tinder or Match Group.
